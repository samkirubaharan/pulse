# Food and breaks

Meals and beverages are included with all passes. The exact inclusions vary by pass type but the baseline is: keynotes, sessions, networking, meals/beverages, and swag bags.

## Named food and break moments (Day 1)

- **Registration + Breakfast** — opening slot
- **"Bites & Breakthrough"** — lunch hour, timed to overlap with informal discussion
- **"Coffee & Ship"** — afternoon coffee break with a build-focused framing
- **"Chai with Community"** — closing block, tea and conversation

## Day 2

- Morning refreshments before the talks
- Lunch window before the 1:00 PM Buildathon kickoff
- Snacks and beverages available during the 4-hour build block

The site's summary: *"delicious food and drinks throughout the day."*

## Dietary requirements

Specific options (vegetarian, vegan, Jain, halal, kosher, allergen-aware) are **not listed on the site**. The site's standing instruction is:

**Action:** Email **team@theproductfolks.com** ahead of the event with your dietary requirement. The earlier, the better — catering decisions firm up closer to the date.
