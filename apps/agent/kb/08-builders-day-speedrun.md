# Builders' Day — Speedrun Buildathon

Day 2 (Sunday, 22 November 2026) is for individual builders. The site also styles this day as **"Speedrun · UnConference '26"**.

## Format

- **Solo only.** One builder, one build. **No teams.**
- The framing is explicitly *"like a one-person company."*
- Morning: talks
- **Buildathon kicks off at 1:00 PM** and runs for roughly 4 hours

## Problem statements

- Problems are contributed by **founders, operators, and VCs** and published on the **"Request for Builders" board**
- Builders may **pick a problem from the board** or **submit their own** problem statement

## Prizes

- Cash and credits worth **$20,000+** in total
- **$5,000 cash** component
- Partner credits distributed at the venue (see the AI & Partner Credits topic)

## Who gets in

Buy the **Builders Pass at ₹1,999** — it is the only direct-purchase pass and the only route into Day 2. The pass covers:
- Day 2 talks

## What to bring

- Your laptop and dev environment
- Any API keys you already use (the partner credits unlock in-venue on Sunday)
- An open problem you'd like to tackle, in case the board topics don't grab you
