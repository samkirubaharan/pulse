# Agenda and session format

The agenda is **curated by the organisers**, not run as an open marketplace. There is no general open call for session proposals from attendees (not confirmed on site beyond the absence of a proposal mechanism).

## Day 1 — Leaders' Day (Sat 21 Nov)

- **7 stage panels**, including:
  - "Era of Intelligence"
  - AI competitive differentiation
  - Full-stack AI building
  - AI moats
  - Product–engineering–design collaboration
  - Voice agents
  - Production AI challenges
- **5 hands-on workshops**
- **11 Brain Date roundtables** (see the Brain Dates topic for detail)
- Named sessions and moments:
  - Registration + Breakfast
  - "Bites & Breakthrough" lunch
  - "Coffee & Ship"
  - "Touch Grass & Serendipity Zone"
  - "Chai with Community" closing
- **After-party:** "Bits n Atoms, powered by T-Hub"

## Day 2 — Builders' Day / Speedrun (Sun 22 Nov)

- Morning talks
- **Buildathon starts at 1:00 PM** — a 4-hour solo build competition
- Problem statements come from founders, operators, and VCs on the "Request for Builders" board
- Builders may also submit their own problem statement
- Prizes: **cash and credits worth $20,000+**, including a **$5K cash component**

## Session proposals

- **General attendees:** no open call for proposals — the stage agenda is pre-set
- **Builders Day:** pick a problem from the Request for Builders board, or propose your own
- **Speaker or facilitator enquiries:** email team@theproductfolks.com

## Preparation

The site's advice: come with an open mind and curiosity, review the agenda in advance, dress business casual, and skip the business cards — the networking format is designed around genuine connection, not card exchange.
