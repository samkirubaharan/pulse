# Brain Dates

Brain Dates are the organisers' flagship curated networking format on Day 1.

**What they are:** Small-group roundtables of **8–10 people**, moderated by an industry leader on a specific topic.

**How many:** **11 Brain Date tables** are scheduled across Day 1.

**Who can book one:** **Reserve Pass holders only.** Each Reserve Pass includes **one Brain Date seat**. Community, Tribe, and Builders Pass holders do not get a reserved Brain Date seat.

**Why the gating:** The format only works at 8–10 people per table. Capping access by pass tier is how the organisers keep the ratio tight.

## How to pick which Brain Date to join

The site lists the 11 table topics in the agenda section. Reserve Pass holders are expected to indicate preferences ahead of the event (not confirmed on site — the exact booking mechanism is not published). If you hold a Reserve Pass and have not received instructions, email team@theproductfolks.com.

## What a Brain Date looks like in practice

- A moderator opens with a 3–5 minute framing of the problem or theme
- Attendees go around and introduce their angle in 1–2 sentences each
- Open discussion for ~45 minutes
- Closing round: one takeaway each

The framing is deliberately peer-to-peer, not Q&A — the moderator's job is to keep the table balanced, not to lecture.

## Not a Brain Date, but similar

If you do not have a Reserve Pass, the Day 1 agenda includes the **"Touch Grass & Serendipity Zone"** and **"Chai with Community"** blocks, which are open informal networking windows available to all pass holders.
