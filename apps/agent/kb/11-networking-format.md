# Networking format

The networking programme is deliberately designed — multiple formats run in parallel so attendees can pick the depth and style that suits them.

## Scale

- **350+ product leaders** expected on Day 1
- Attendee set skewed to founders and senior product/tech/design/growth leaders

## Formats

### Brain Dates (curated — Reserve Pass only)
- 8–10 person moderated roundtables
- 11 tables across Day 1
- See the Brain Dates topic for detail

### Touch Grass & Serendipity Zone
- Informal break / networking block
- Open to all pass holders
- Designed for unstructured, serendipitous meetings — the name is literal

### Chai with Community
- Closing block on Day 1
- Tea, conversation, soft wind-down after the stage programme

### After-party — "Bits n Atoms"
- Day 1 evening
- Powered by **T-Hub**
- The main social moment of the event

### Side quests and activations
- **"Flip your IDs"** — side quest during lunch
- **Stripe Press book collection quest**
- **SuperErgo chair giveaway** via the social hashtag **#TPFUNCONF26**
- **FigJam wall for AI takes** — collaborative board where attendees post their hot takes

## Conference etiquette (per the site)

- **No business cards.** The focus is on genuine connection, not contact scraping.
- Business casual dress.
- In-person only — there is no virtual / hybrid overlay.

## Introverts' survival kit

- Pick two anchor moments (one meal, one break) and have a topic you want to ask about ready
- Brain Date (if you have a Reserve Pass) is pre-structured — less load than open floor
- The Serendipity Zone is open floor but small enough to re-encounter the same faces

## Post-event

No Slack community, shared notes page, or alumni forum is mentioned on the site *(not confirmed on site)*. If you want to stay connected with someone, swap details before you leave.
