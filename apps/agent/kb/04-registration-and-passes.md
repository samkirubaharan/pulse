# Registration and passes

There are four pass tiers. Three are **invite-only** (and currently sold out); one is direct-purchase.

| Pass | Price | Status | Key inclusions |
|---|---|---|---|
| Community Pass | not published | **Sold out** | $500 Claude credits, 10+ hrs content, 50+ speakers, 350+ networking |
| Reserve Pass | not published | **Sold out** | $500 Claude + $1,000 AI credits, one Brain Date (curated roundtable) |
| Tribe Pass | not published | **Sold out** | $500 Claude + $1,000 AI credits per person, group pass for 3 |
| **Builders Pass** | **₹1,999** | **Available** | $500 Claude credits, $1M+ total partner credits distributed, $5K cash prizes, Day 2 access |

**How invite-based passes work:**
1. Fill out the online invite-request form on the event page, explaining why you want to attend.
2. The team reviews applications and responds **via email** with a yes or no. **Check your spam folder.**
3. The site notes they receive "thousands of applications every year" — the review is deliberate, not instant. There is no published SLA for a decision.

**Builders Pass is different** — it is directly purchasable at ₹1,999 and grants access to Day 2 (Speedrun). No invite review is required.

**Transferability:** Invites are **non-transferable and cannot be resold** (stated explicitly on the site).

**If you can't attend:** Email team@theproductfolks.com as early as possible so the organisers can manage the guest list. The site does not publish a monetary refund policy for the paid Builders Pass *(not confirmed on site)*.

**Company sponsoring your pass:** Yes — the organisers provide a sponsorship / reimbursement letter you can use for internal approval. A dedicated page lives at `/unconference-26-company-reimbursment`.

**Contact for any registration question:** team@theproductfolks.com.
