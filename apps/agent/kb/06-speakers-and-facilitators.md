## Facilitators vs speakers

- **Speakers** take the stage panels and keynotes.
- **Facilitators** moderate Brain Date roundtables (Day 1) and host the hands-on workshops. The facilitator list overlaps with but is not identical to the speaker list; see the agenda section for the mapping.

## Want to propose yourself as a speaker or facilitator?

Email **team@theproductfolks.com** with your background and the topic you'd like to cover. There is no public submission form, and the 2026 line-up is already deep into confirmation — a late proposal is more likely to land you on a future edition's shortlist than the 2026 stage.

## How to look up a specific session

Use the `search_schedule` tool with a topic keyword (for example *"voice agents"*, *"ai moats"*, *"speedrun"*). The tool returns day, time, room, and — where published — the facilitator name.
