# Post-event resources

The organisers' post-event posture is deliberately minimal. The event is designed for in-room value — what you take away is what you captured while you were there.

## No recordings

- **Sessions are not recorded.** This is called out in the FAQ.
- There is no on-demand video, audio, or transcript library after the event.
- The organisers' framing: in-person attendance is the point.

## What you do take home

- **Swag bag** (contents are deliberately not detailed — "a surprise! You'll find out at the venue")
- **AI credits** on your registered email (see the AI & Partner Credits topic)
  - Claude credits issued Sunday morning to on-time registrants
  - Partner credits distributed in person on Day 2
  - Late registrants (after 19 Nov) get Claude credits after the event concludes
- **Your own notes, FigJam captures, and photos** from the day

## No shared notes or alumni community

The site does not mention:
- A shared notes doc
- A post-event Slack or Discord
- A speaker-slides repository
- An alumni Luma or forum

*(All of the above: not confirmed on site. Absence is noted but may change year-over-year.)*

## If you captured something you want to share back

- **#TPFUNCONF26** is the official social hashtag — the SuperErgo giveaway runs on it
- Posting to LinkedIn or X with the tag is the closest thing to a public shared feed
- Direct outreach to speakers via LinkedIn is the typical path for follow-up conversations

## What if you missed a session?

Because nothing is recorded, your options are:
- Talk to someone who attended that session (the "Chai with Community" block on Day 1 is partly designed for this)
- Follow the speaker on LinkedIn for their own write-up, if they do one
- Email the speaker (or team@theproductfolks.com for an intro)
