# What is the Product (Un)Conference?

The Product (Un)Conference '26 — also styled "Product UnConference 2026" — is The Product Folks' annual gathering for founders and senior leaders in product, tech, design, and growth. The 2026 edition carries the tagline **"Into the Future"** and is sharply focused on where AI is taking Indian product building.

The format is participant-driven, more unconference than traditional conference. It is *not* explicitly branded as classical Open Space Technology on the organiser's site (not confirmed on site), but the shape is similar in spirit: curated panels and workshops interleaved with roundtables, informal networking blocks, and a solo build competition on Day 2.

**At a glance:**
- Two full days across 21–22 November 2026
- 10+ hours of programming each day
- Day 1 is "Leaders' Day"; Day 2 is "Builders' Day / Speedrun"
- In-person only — **sessions are not recorded**
- Dress code is business casual

**Scale reference** (from the 2025 edition):
- 10,000+ registrations
- 350+ leaders attending
- 50+ speakers

The 2026 edition is targeting similar or greater scale. The through-line across both days is AI's future in Indian product and startup ecosystems — the organisers describe the attendee set as the "movers, shakers, and innovators" working on that.

If you want a hands-on, no-recording, high-signal couple of days with other senior product people and builders focused on AI, this is the event. If you want slide-ware you can watch later on YouTube, it is explicitly not that.
