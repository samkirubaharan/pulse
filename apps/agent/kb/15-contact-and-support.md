# Contact and support

The organisers run a deliberately lean support footprint. There is **no phone number, live chat, or ticketing system** on the site. Email is the single channel.

## Primary channels

- **General questions:** **team@theproductfolks.com**
- **Co-founder direct line:** **suhas@theproductfolks.com** (Suhas Motwani — use sparingly; general queries should go to the team address)
- **Volunteer applications:** Google Form linked from the main site
- **Speaker / facilitator proposals:** team@theproductfolks.com
- **Sponsor / partnership inquiries:** team@theproductfolks.com
- **Visa assistance requests:** team@theproductfolks.com (see the Visa topic for what to include)

## Response expectations

The site notes the team receives "thousands of applications every year" — treat email response times as **"as soon as practical"** rather than instant. There is **no published SLA** *(not confirmed on site)*.

- Check your **spam folder** for replies — this is explicitly flagged in the FAQ
- For time-sensitive items (dietary needs, visa letters, pass transfer refusal appeals), email as early as possible

## On-site support

- **No phone number** published for the day of the event
- **No on-site helpdesk** address or contact is disclosed on the site *(not confirmed on site)*
- Expect a registration desk at the venue — venue address is shared with confirmed attendees only

## What to escalate (and how)

Use the `escalate_to_human` tool when:
- A question falls outside what the KB can ground
- The attendee asks for a human
- The matter is time-sensitive (day-of logistics, missed credit distribution, access issues)

Every escalation generates a ticket ID that the attendee should quote when following up by email.
