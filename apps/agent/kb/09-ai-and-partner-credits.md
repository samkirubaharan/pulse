# AI and partner credits

Every attendee receives AI credits. The amount and mix depend on the pass tier and the registration email you used.

## Claude credits (Anthropic)

- **$500 Claude credits** if you register with a **work / company email**
- **$100 Claude credits** if you register with a **personal email** (e.g., Gmail)
- Credits are issued to the **exact email you used to register** — not a second address

## Where the larger AI credit pool lives

- Reserve and Tribe Pass holders get an **additional $1,000 of AI credits per person** on top of the Claude allocation
- Builders Pass holders get access to the partner credit pool at the venue (see below)

## Partner credits

Distributed on **Day 2 at the venue**. Providers include:

- **Apify**
- **CrustData**
- **Divyam.ai**
- **ElevenLabs**
- **Plivo**
- **Replit**
- **Token Factory**
- **CodeRabbit**

The headline framing on the site is **"$1M+ total partner credits distributed"** across the attendee base.

## When credits are issued

- **Registered on or before 19 Nov:** Claude credits issued **Sunday morning** to the registered email. Check the Credits page at **12 PM** to confirm.
- **Registered after 19 Nov:** Credits are issued **after the event concludes**.
- **Partner credits (Apify, Crustdata, etc.):** handed out **in person on Day 2** — you must be physically present. (not confirmed on site — the exact distribution window and what happens if you arrive late is not published.)

## Troubleshooting

- Credit didn't land? First check the inbox for the email you used at registration, then spam, then contact team@theproductfolks.com.
- Used the wrong email? Email the team **before** the Sunday morning cutoff — once credits are issued to an address, re-routing is not guaranteed.
