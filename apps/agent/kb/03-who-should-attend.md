# Who should attend

The Product (Un)Conference 2026 is curated for a specific audience. The organisers vet applications rather than opening general registration for most pass tiers.

**Primary audience (Day 1 — Leaders' Day):**
- Founders of product-led companies
- Senior leaders in product, tech, design, or growth
- People specifically working on or thinking about **AI's future in India**
- The site frames the attendee set as the "movers, shakers, and innovators of the ecosystem"

**Day 2 — Builders' Day (Speedrun):**
- Individual builders and developers
- Solo competition only — **no teams**. The framing is "one builder, one build, like a one-person company"
- Attendees pick a problem from the "Request for Builders" board (contributed by founders, operators, VCs) or submit their own

**Scale and seniority (2025 reference):**
- 350+ product leaders attended
- 50+ speakers
- 10,000+ registrations submitted

**Not a fit if:**
- You are early in your career and looking for a learning-only experience — this is a peer-level event
- You want recorded sessions to review later (there are none)
- You want a team hackathon on Day 2 (solo only)

**FAQ echo from the site:** *"Is this the right event for me?"* → *"Yes if you're a founder or senior leader in product/tech/design/growth focused on AI in India."*

**Minimum age:** Not stated on the site *(not confirmed on site)*. If you have a question about age or bringing someone with you, contact team@theproductfolks.com before applying.
