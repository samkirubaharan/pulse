# Visa assistance for international attendees

A dedicated page on the organiser site (`/unconference-26-visa-assistance`) covers visa logistics for international attendees. The summary below reflects what is published there.

## The short version

- **Most nationalities** are eligible for an **Indian e-Visa** that covers conference / business / tourism travel
- Apply through the **Government of India e-Visa website**
- Typical approval time: **3–5 working days** (plan well ahead — do not leave this to the last week)

## Documents you'll need

- **Passport valid for at least 6 months beyond your arrival date** in India
- **Return ticket proof**
- **Conference invitation letter** — issued by the organisers to approved international participants

## How to request your invitation letter

Email **team@theproductfolks.com** with:

- Your **full name as it appears in your passport**
- Your **passport number**
- Your **nationality**
- Your **expected travel dates**

The team will reply with the formal invitation letter you can attach to your e-Visa application and / or present on arrival.

## Who this applies to

- International attendees travelling to Bengaluru specifically for the event
- Both leadership-track (Day 1) and builder-track (Day 2) attendees

## What is *not* covered on the visa assistance page

- **Customs / immigration tips on arrival** — not stated *(not confirmed on site)*
- **Currency of pricing** — some pass components are quoted in USD (credits), the Builders Pass is in INR; the site does not consolidate this into one FX note
- **Time zones for credit distribution** — the Sunday morning Claude credit issuance follows local IST; the site does not call this out for international attendees

## Flights and arrival

Nearest airport: **Kempegowda International Airport (BLR), Bengaluru**. Allow buffer time — November is a busy inbound month into BLR.

## Company reimbursement for international attendees

The sponsorship / reimbursement letter at `/unconference-26-company-reimbursment` is usable for internal expense approval regardless of your country of origin. Combine it with the invitation letter when you file.
